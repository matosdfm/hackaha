01 / ABOUT

Prague's day for
independent work.

Hackaha is a community-run Thursday coworking day for people building their own things. Apps, startups, experiments, hardware, side projects — bring the work that matters to you.

No talks. No pitches. No productivity theatre. Just a room full of curious people, a clear goal for the day, and a chance to demo what changed.
